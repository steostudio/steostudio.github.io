On This Publication

The Evening Reel is an independent weekly of film criticism, published on Sunday evenings and delivered, where possible, by hand. We review nothing we have not watched in full, and we watch nothing twice except under protest. Correspondence — particularly the disagreeing kind — is warmly received.
